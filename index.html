About Me

I'm a senior software engineer and architect with more than 20 years of experience in gaming, fintech and telecommunications.

My core expertise is Rust, C/C++ and distributed real-time systems. I have led engineering as a CTO and as a founder, designing scalable architectures and CI/CD pipelines while mentoring high-performing teams.

I care about clean code, testing, automation and open source. In my spare time I'm writing a book on building a NES emulator in Rust from scratch, and I build the tools I use for my own music practice. Away from the keyboard, I play piano and guitar, paint and sketch.
